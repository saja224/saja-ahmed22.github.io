Public Sentiment on Egypt’s Youth Travel Initiative

What public comments on the “Know Your Country” initiative’s posts reveal about access, fairness and who the trips leave out.

  • Team project
  • Python · Pandas · Google Colab
  • Egyptian Arabic comments
الرحلة الأخيرة كانت ممتازة، شكرًا لكل القائمين.Positive
أتمنى يكون في رحلة لواحة سيوة قريبًا.Neutral
الرحلات جميلة، لكن التقديم صعب والمعلومات مش واضحة.Negative

01 · The question

What does the public really think, without running a survey?

Egypt’s Ministry of Youth and Sports runs travel initiatives that help young people discover their country, including “Know Your Country” (اعرف بلدك). The team set out to understand public opinion of these trips from what people write on social media, as a faster alternative to surveys, and to turn it into input for improving the programs.

02 · Data & approach

Real comments, read and labeled

  • Data: public comments in Egyptian Arabic, exported from the initiative’s social-media posts.
  • Processing: loaded and prepared in Python with Pandas, in Google Colab.
  • Labeling: each comment classified as positive, negative or neutral, then read for recurring themes.

Comments are quoted below as text only, without names, usernames or profile images.

03 · What people said

The themes behind the negative comments

  • Fairness of selection

    People feel places go to the same people, and ask how participants are chosen.

    دايمًا بنقدم وبيتحجز لناس تانية، فين الشفافية؟

    “We always apply and the places go to other people. Where’s the transparency?”

  • Registration and capacity

    Trips fill very quickly and the application process feels hard.

    مش مصدقة إن الرحلة اتقفلت بعد نص ساعة!

    “I can’t believe the trip closed after half an hour!”

  • Which governorates are included

    Commenters from some governorates say trips never reach them.

    في محافظات دايمًا مش بيطلع لها رحلات، وده مش عدل.

    “Some governorates never get trips, and that isn’t fair.”

  • Communication and schedules

    People want trips announced earlier and dates made clear.

    مفيش تواصل واضح، مش عارفين المواعيد.

    “There’s no clear communication; we don’t know the dates.”

  • People outside the age limit

    Older adults and families ask to take part in trips aimed at young people.

    عايزين نروح معاكم بس إحنا فوق 45، مش كبار يعني!

    “We want to come with you, but we’re over 45 — that’s not old!”

    نفسي في رحلات للأسر وكبار السن.

    “I wish there were trips for families and older people.”

04 · Insight

The complaints are about access, not the trips themselves

Many negative comments begin by praising the trips, then object to how places are allocated, how fast they fill and which governorates are included. Alongside these, a recurring request comes from people outside the eligible age group, including older adults and families, who want to take part too.

05 · Recommendation

Open a path for older age groups

  1. Extend eligibility, or create a separate program

    Offer older adults and families their own trips, rather than leaving them outside the current age limit.

06 · Limitations

What these comments cannot say

  • People who comment on social media are not a representative sample of all participants.
  • The comments come from one initiative’s posts, so the findings may not apply to other programs.
  • Sentiment labels reflect the team’s classification of each comment.
